Fish Jigsaw Helper

Independent fan-made helper for the Metin2 Fish Jigsaw (Balık Yapboz) mini-game. Analyze the 4×6 board, place pieces, and see shortest finishing paths and chest estimates (B / M / S in English; B / O / K in Turkish). Not affiliated with Metin2 or Gameforge.

Read how to play, rules, the fishing event calendar (Ezel/Barbaros, Marmara/Bagjanamu, Charon/Lucifer/Safir/Star), or contact us on Discord.
