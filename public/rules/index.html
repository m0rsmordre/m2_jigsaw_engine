Rules

  • Fill the 4×6 board with pieces. Pieces cannot be rotated.
  • Each turn you receive a piece; place it legally or discard (pass).
  • Deluxe (3×2) is limited by stock; at 0 stock deluxe cannot be used.
  • Golden Fish Chest (B): 10 moves or fewer.
  • Golden Fish Chest (O): 11–24 moves.
  • Golden Fish Chest (K): 25 or more moves.

This is an independent fan-made project. It has no official affiliation with Metin2, Gameforge, or their publishers.
