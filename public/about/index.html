About

Balık Yapboz Yardımcısı is a fan-made tool that analyzes the Fish Jigsaw board and suggests short finishing paths using a precomputed tiling tablebase.

Use Play for interactive analysis, Calendar for fishing event times, and Contact for Discord support.
