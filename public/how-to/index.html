How to play

Balık Yapboz Yardımcısı helps you plan Fish Jigsaw placements and estimate chests.

  1. Open the Play page and select the piece in your hand.
  2. Left-click the board to place the piece on a legal cell.
  3. Use Undo or the Left Arrow key to reverse the last move.
  4. Check the analysis panel for the suggested move and shortest continuations.
  5. Click a continuation scenario to apply its next recommended placement.
  6. Track deluxe stock so deluxe pieces are only used when available.

Chest tiers: B ≤10 moves, O 11–24, K ≥25. Independent fan tool — not affiliated with Metin2 / Gameforge.
